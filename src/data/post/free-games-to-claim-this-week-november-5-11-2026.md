New free PC games land every Thursday — and this week's swap (November 5 – 11, 2026) brings an ocean-survival city builder to the Epic Games Store. **Sorry We're Closed** leaves the free lineup Thursday morning, replaced by **Flotsam** and **Idle Champions of the Forgotten Realms**. Below are this week's freebies, their claim deadlines, and — if you'd rather play something _right now_ — free browser games that need no download at all.

**One important note first:** the games below are full PC downloads you claim through the Epic Games Store or Steam — they are _not_ browser games, and you'll need a free Epic account to claim them. If you want instant play with zero installs, skip to the [browser games](#want-instant-play-free-browser-games) at the bottom.

## Epic Games Store — Free November 5 – 11

### Flotsam (free from Nov 5)

The headline giveaway of the week: an ocean-survival city builder where you guide a crew of drifters across a flooded world, salvaging wreckage to keep your floating town alive. Normally $24.99 — free to keep from November 5 through November 12. _(Free November 5 – 12, as listed.)_

### Idle Champions of the Forgotten Realms (free from Nov 5)

The Dungeons & Dragons idle battler joins the lineup the same day — assemble a party of D&D heroes and let them fight through the Forgotten Realms while you're away. Free-to-play, free to claim November 5 – 12. _(Free November 5 – 12, as listed.)_

### Last chance: Sorry We're Closed (ends Nov 5, 16:00 UTC)

The survival horror leaves Epic's free lineup Thursday at 16:00 UTC (11 AM ET). You play Michelle, a shopkeeper trapped between the human world and a demon realm — angelic, demonic, and normal weapons included. Normally $24.99 — claim it before the swap or it's gone. _(Claim deadline November 5, 16:00 UTC, as listed.)_

### Also on Epic until Nov 8

Three smaller giveaways run through the weekend: **Gladiator: Blades of Fury** (medieval arena brawler, normally $9.99), **Balloon Strike**, and **Epic World – Hunt for Quest** — all claimable until November 8. _(Deadlines as listed.)_

## Steam — Last Gambit, free to keep before November 13

A chess roguelite that's free in Early Access right now: add **Last Gambit** to your Steam library before its 1.0 launch on November 13 and you keep it for free, its developer says. If you like your strategy with a twist, this is the week's easiest win. _(Keep-forever offer ends with the 1.0 launch on November 13, as announced.)_

## Prime Gaming — Trash Goblin, Kiborg, and last-call horror

Prime members still have **Trash Goblin** (a cozy shopkeeping game, Epic Games Store code, through December 2 — $19.99 value) and **Kiborg** (roguelite action, Amazon Games App). Horror fans get two more weeks: **Vampire: The Masquerade – Swansong** through November 18 and **Ad Infinitum** through November 25.

**Today only:** **DOOM (2016)** and **Five Nights at Freddy's: Into the Pit** both expire November 4 — if you're reading this on Wednesday, claim them now. _(Deadlines as listed by the stores; claim early — giveaway end dates can shift.)_
