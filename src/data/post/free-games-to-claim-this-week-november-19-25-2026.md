That listing came from early Epic feed data, but the game never appeared on Epic's free games page — only Mark of the Deep went free that week. The sections below have been corrected.

Thursday is the biggest free-games day of the month so far. The Epic Games Store swaps its weekly giveaway on November 19 at 11 AM ET, and this time the headline is the pirate Metroidvania **Mark of the Deep** (normally $19.99) — free to keep until November 26. Amazon Prime members get two more the same day, **Werewolf: The Apocalypse – Earthblood** and **Clem**, through the Luna claims page. Below are this week's freebies, their claim deadlines, and — if you'd rather play something *right now* — free Christmas browser games that need no download at all.

**One important note first:** the games below are full PC downloads you claim through the Epic Games Store or Amazon Luna — they are _not_ browser games, and you'll need a free Epic account to claim them. If you want instant play with zero installs, skip to the [Christmas browser games](#want-instant-play-free-christmas-browser-games) at the bottom.

## Epic Games Store — Free November 19 — 26

### Mark of the Deep (free from Nov 19)

A pirate Metroidvania with soulslike combat. You play a pirate chasing the crew that betrayed you across a cursed archipelago, unlocking new abilities that open up earlier areas, and fighting bosses that punish button-mashing. It is the kind of game most likely to surprise you. Normally $19.99 — free to keep from November 19 at 11 AM ET through November 26 at 11 AM ET. _(As announced by Epic; free November 19 — 26.)_

[Claim Mark of the Deep free on Epic](https://store.epicgames.com/en-US/p/mark-of-the-deep-f7dc08)

### Last chance: Knightica (ends Nov 19, 11 AM ET)

The roguelike autobattler from Goblinz Studio leaves Epic's free lineup Thursday morning. You recruit units, place them on the battlefield, and hunt for the synergies that carry your warband through each run. Normally $14.99 — claim it before 11 AM ET on November 19 or it's gone. _(As listed by Epic; free November 12 — 19.)_

[Claim Knightica free on Epic](https://store.epicgames.com/en-US/p/knightica-3be45c)

### Already on Epic's calendar

Epic's own promotions feed already lists what's further out: **TerraScape** from November 21, and **LISA: The Definitive Edition**, **Monument Valley**, plus **Ghostrunner 2** from December 10. Dates on a feed can still shift before a window opens, so treat these as a heads-up, not a promise — we'll confirm each one in the weekly roundup when its Thursday arrives.

## Prime Gaming — Free November 19

Amazon's November lineup is seven PC games in four batches, and the third batch lands Thursday, November 19. Prime members claim these on the Luna claims page; the codes redeem on the Epic Games Store and the games are yours to keep.

### Werewolf: The Apocalypse – Earthblood (Nov 19)

The action RPG where you play Cahal, a werewolf exiled from his tribe, fighting the oil corporation destroying the forests of the Pacific Northwest. The hook is the three-form combat: slip past patrols as a wolf, sneak as a human, and tear through rooms as the Crinos beast when stealth fails. It is a pulpy, full-blooded action game — and a very different flavor from the horror headlining Epic this week. _(As announced by Amazon; free from November 19.)_

### Clem (Nov 19)

A dark, story-driven puzzle adventure, and the quieter half of Thursday's Prime drop. You solve environmental puzzles and follow a strange, melancholy story — the kind of game that sticks with you longer than its playtime suggests. If you like narrative games that leave you thinking afterwards, this is your pick of the Prime batch. _(As announced by Amazon; free from November 19.)_

### Coming next: Arcadia Fallen (Nov 24)

The month's final Prime game arrives Tuesday, November 24, on the Amazon Games App rather than Epic — a modern fantasy visual novel RPG. It is the only November Prime game that redeems outside the Epic Games Store, so make sure you have the Amazon Games App ready. _(As announced by Amazon; free from November 24.)_

### Last chances on Prime

Two November Prime games are about to expire. **Vampire: The Masquerade – Swansong**, the narrative RPG, must be claimed by **November 18**. **Ad Infinitum**, the World War I-set first-person horror adventure, must be claimed by **November 25**. Still claimable from earlier batches: **Jurassic World Evolution 2** ($59.99) and **Radiolight** ($14.99), plus **WRC Generations** and **Squirrel with a Gun**. Across all seven November Prime games, the month's haul is worth roughly $206 at regular prices.
