Halloween is 17 days away, and this year you can stock your scary-games shelf for $0. Epic Games Store and Prime Gaming are both running Halloween-season giveaways right now — full games you keep forever, not trials. The catch: every freebie has a claim deadline, and the first one expires **tomorrow night**. Here's what's free, when it ends, and how to grab it.

## Epic Games Store: Out of Sight (horror) + TerraScape — deadline Oct 15

This week's Epic pair is live **until Thursday, October 15 at 8:00 AM PT (11:00 PM Beijing time)**:

- **Out of Sight** (normally $14.99) — a rare second-person horror puzzle adventure. You play Sophie, a young blind girl trapped in a dark mansion who sees the world only through her teddy bear's eyes. Creep, hide, and solve your way out while her captors hunt you. Easily the standout Halloween freebie this week.
- **TerraScape** (normally $16.99) — not horror, but a cozy card-driven city-builder worth grabbing in the same checkout. Two separate $0 orders, both stay in your Epic library forever.

Two things to know: adding a game to your wishlist or cart does **not** claim it — you must complete the free checkout. And **next week (Oct 15–22)** Epic rotates to **Bad Cheese**, a psychological horror game in 1920s rubber-hose cartoon style, plus spy puzzler **Agent A: A Puzzle in Disguise**. Mark your calendar.

## Prime Gaming: a whole month of horror (needs Prime)

Prime members get 10 free PC games across October, claimed on Amazon's Luna/Prime Gaming page and redeemed on Epic, GOG, or the Amazon Games app. The horror picks:

- **Five Nights at Freddy's: Into the Pit** (Epic) — claim by **Nov 4**. The pixel-art FNAF spin-off, Overwhelmingly Positive on Steam.
- **DOOM (2016)** (GOG) — claim by **Nov 4**. Not Halloween-specific, but the definitive warm-up shooter.
- **Conarium** (GOG) — claim by **Nov 11**. Lovecraft-inspired horror from Zoetrope Interactive.
- **Vampire: The Masquerade – Swansong** (Epic) — unlocks **Oct 15**.
- **Ad Infinitum** (Epic) — unlocks **Oct 22**. A World War I nightmare, perfect Halloween-week material.
- **WarBreeds** (GOG) — claim by **Jan 8, 2027**, the longest window of the bunch.

Once claimed, the games stay in your library even if you cancel Prime later.
