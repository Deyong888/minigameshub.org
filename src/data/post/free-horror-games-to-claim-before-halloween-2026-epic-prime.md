Halloween is 16 days away, and this year you can stock your scary-games shelf for $0. Epic Games Store and Prime Gaming are both running Halloween-season giveaways — full games you keep forever, not trials. Every freebie has a claim deadline, so here's what's free right now, when it ends, and how to grab it.

## Epic Games Store: Bad Cheese (horror) + Agent A — free until Oct 22

This week's Epic pair is live **from Thursday, October 15 at 8:00 AM PT (11:00 PM Beijing time) until Thursday, October 22** at the same hour:

- **Bad Cheese** (normally $12.99) — the Halloween pick. A psychological horror game drawn in the style of 1920s rubber-hose cartoons: cheerful-looking vintage animation wrapped around something genuinely unsettling. Two honest caveats: player reviews are mixed (the art style wins universal praise, the gameplay divides opinion), and it is **Windows-only**, so Mac players are out of luck. Free is still free — judge it yourself.
- **Agent A: A Puzzle in Disguise** (normally $19.99) — not horror, but a well-loved 2019 spy-themed escape-room puzzler (mostly positive player reviews) worth grabbing in the same checkout. Works on Windows and macOS. Two separate $0 orders, both stay in your Epic library forever.

Two things to know: adding a game to your wishlist or cart does **not** claim it — you must complete the free $0 checkout for each title. And these are **PC games via the Epic Games Store**: claiming keeps them in your library, but playing requires a download and install. If downloads aren't your thing, skip to the browser section below.

Prime members get 10 free PC games across October, claimed on Amazon's Luna/Prime Gaming page and redeemed on Epic, GOG, or the Amazon Games app. The horror picks:

- **Five Nights at Freddy's: Into the Pit** (Epic) — claim by **Nov 4**. The pixel-art FNAF spin-off, Overwhelmingly Positive on Steam.
- **DOOM (2016)** (GOG) — claim by **Nov 4**. Not Halloween-specific, but the definitive warm-up shooter.
- **Conarium** (GOG) — claim by **Nov 11**. Lovecraft-inspired horror from Zoetrope Interactive.

Once claimed, the games stay in your library even if you cancel Prime later.
