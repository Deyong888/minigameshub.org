## Epic Games Store — Free October 22–28

### Sker Ritual (free from Oct 22)
A round-based co-op survival FPS set on a haunted island, from the creators of Sker Ritual's spiritual predecessor Maid of Sker. Team up with up to 4 players, fend off the Quiet Ones, and survive the miracles. A perfect Halloween-week pickup for co-op groups. *(As announced by Epic; free from October 22.)*

### Home (free from Oct 22)
A minimalist pixel-art horror adventure about a man who wakes up in a stranger's house with no memory of how he got there. Every choice shapes the story — and the ending. Short, atmospheric, and ideal for a single spooky evening. *(As announced by Epic; free from October 22.)*

### High on Life (claim by Oct 28)
The comedy FPS from Squanch Games where your guns literally talk to you. A bounty-hunter campaign across alien worlds with genuinely funny writing — one of the bigger names Epic has given away this month. *(Claim deadline October 28, as listed.)*

### Hue (claim by Oct 28)
A clever puzzle-platformer where you shift the world's background color to make obstacles appear and disappear. Stylish, relaxing, and a great palette cleanser between horror titles. *(Claim deadline October 28, as listed.)*

### Last chance: RiMS Racing (ends Oct 21)
Today is the final day to claim the motorcycle racing sim **RiMS Racing** on Epic — it rotates out when the new freebies arrive on October 22. If you haven't grabbed it yet, do it now.
