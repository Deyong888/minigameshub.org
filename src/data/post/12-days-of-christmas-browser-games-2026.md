Every game is free, plays instantly in your browser, and needs no download or sign-up.

Here's how it works. Each morning, the homepage spotlight switches to that day's game. Play it, enjoy it, and come back tomorrow for the next one. On December 12 we count up which category got the most love, and the strongest game from the winning category takes over the homepage spotlight for Christmas week, December 13–25. Think of it as an advent calendar where you unwrap a game instead of a chocolate.

## Action: Santa on the run

**Dec 1 — [Santa Snow Runner](https://minigameshub.org/game/UAE5R-santa-snow-runner).** Our opening game, and the title says it all: Santa, running, snow. Guide Santa through snowy landscapes, dodge obstacles, and collect gifts along the way. Running games are our strongest category, and this one wears its Christmas theme on its sleeve.

**Dec 4 — [Mr Santa Run 2](https://minigameshub.org/game/6A631-mr-santa-run-2).** Santa's running again in the sequel to the holiday runner — same simple idea, new course to learn. A good second helping of action once the first day's snow settles.

**Dec 7 — [Santabalt](https://minigameshub.org/game/FZBGMS-santabalt).** The week's wildcard: a Santa action game with a name that gives nothing away. Half the fun is finding out what it is — come back on December 7 and see.

**Dec 10 — [Reindeer Escape](https://minigameshub.org/game/LGTLP1-reindeer-escape).** The reindeer take their turn in the spotlight. An escape adventure with Santa's sleigh team — the action category's closing act before the finale weekend.

## Puzzle: quiet nights by the fire

**Dec 2 — [Jewel Christmas Mania](https://minigameshub.org/game/MIHL1-jewel-christmas-mania).** Swap and match shimmering jewels against a cheerful holiday backdrop of snowmen and gingerbread. Match-3 is the most relaxing genre we carry, and this is the most complete of the three Christmas Jewel games on the site.

**Dec 5 — [Mahjong At Home - Xmas Edition](https://minigameshub.org/game/0H1AO-mahjong-at-home-xmas-edition).** Classic mahjong solitaire dressed up for the holidays. Slow, thoughtful, and perfect with a mug of something warm — the calmest game in the whole calendar.

**Dec 8 — [Christmas Brain Teasers](https://minigameshub.org/game/373A1B-christmas-brain-teasers).** A set of holiday-themed puzzles and brain teasers. Short, sharp, and shareable — the one to send to a friend who thinks they're clever.

**Dec 11 — [Jewel Christmas](https://minigameshub.org/game/TSA82-jewel-christmas).** The puzzle category closes with the original Jewel Christmas — same sparkling match-3 idea as day two, a different board to master. Compare your scores across the two and settle which Jewel game is really yours.

## Winter sports: out in the snow

**Dec 3 — [Ski King 2022](https://minigameshub.org/game/3K1I6-ski-king-2022).** Ski down exhilarating slopes, collect coins, and outrun an avalanche. Skiing is the most Christmassy winter sport there is, and the avalanche chase is the hook that keeps you hitting restart.

**Dec 6 — [Snowball Rush 3D](https://minigameshub.org/game/2U9A13-snowball-rush-3d-1).** A 3D snowball run for the Saturday crowd — the weekend slot, and the most frantic game in the winter sports set. Best played when you have nowhere to be.

**Dec 9 — [Snowboard King 2024](https://minigameshub.org/game/58045-snowboard-king-2024).** The snowboard answer to Ski King: same slopes, different board, different lines down the mountain. If you liked day three, day nine is your rematch.

**Dec 12 — [Slalom Ski Simulator](https://minigameshub.org/game/IISR2-slalom-ski-simulator).** The finale, and the most technical game in the calendar: thread the gates, keep your line, and don't clip a flag. A fittingly precise way to close twelve days of play.
