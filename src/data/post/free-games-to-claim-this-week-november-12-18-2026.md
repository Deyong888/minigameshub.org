New free PC games land every Thursday — and this week's rotation (November 12 – 18, 2026) has a little of everything. Epic Games Store headlines with the roguelike autobattler **Knightica**, while Amazon Prime members get the big one: **Jurassic World Evolution 2**, a $59.99 park builder, free to keep. Below are this week's freebies, their claim deadlines, and — if you'd rather play something _right now_ — free Christmas browser games that need no download at all.

**One important note first:** the games below are full PC downloads you claim through the Epic Games Store or Amazon Luna — they are _not_ browser games, and you'll need a free Epic account to claim them. If you want instant play with zero installs, skip to the [Christmas browser games](#want-instant-play-free-christmas-browser-games) at the bottom.

## Epic Games Store — Free November 12 – 19

### Knightica (free from Nov 12)

The headline giveaway of the week: a roguelike autobattler from Goblinz Studio where you recruit units, place them on the battlefield, and hunt for the synergies that carry your warband through each run. Normally $14.99 — free to keep from November 12 at 11 AM ET through November 19 at 11 AM ET. _(As announced by Epic; free November 12 – 19.)_

### Last chance: Flotsam (ends Nov 12, 11 AM ET)

The floating city builder leaves Epic's free lineup Thursday morning. Guide your drifters across an ocean of garbage, salvage ruins, and keep your town afloat. Normally $24.99 — claim it before 11 AM ET on November 12 or it's gone. _(Claim deadline November 12, 11 AM ET, as listed.)_

### Last chance: Idle Champions bonus pack (ends Nov 12, 11 AM ET)

The Dungeons & Dragons idle game itself is free-to-play, but Epic's bonus pack — over $100 worth of in-game content — disappears with the Thursday swap. Same 11 AM ET deadline as Flotsam. Mobile players get a separate farewell: **Chants of Sennaar**, the acclaimed puzzle adventure, is free on the Epic Games Store mobile app until November 12.

## Prime Gaming — Free November 12

Amazon's November lineup is staggered across the month, and November 12 is its biggest day so far. Claim these from Amazon Luna with an active Prime membership; the codes redeem on the Epic Games Store and the games are yours to keep.

### Jurassic World Evolution 2 (Nov 12)

The headline Prime freebie of the month: Frontier's dinosaur park management sim, normally $59.99. Build the park, breed the dinosaurs, and try not to let the T. rex eat the guests. Available from November 12 on the Epic Games Store. _(As announced by Amazon; free from November 12.)_

### Radiolight (Nov 12)

A first-person investigative thriller set in the 1980s — explore, solve the mystery, and don't trust the dark. Normally $14.99, also redeemable on the Epic Games Store from November 12. _(As announced by Amazon; free from November 12.)_

### Already live, and coming next

Two November Prime games are claimable right now, since November 5: rally racer **WRC Generations** and the gloriously silly **Squirrel with a Gun**. Coming next are **Werewolf: The Apocalypse – Earthblood** and **Clem** on November 19, and **Arcadia Fallen** on November 24.
