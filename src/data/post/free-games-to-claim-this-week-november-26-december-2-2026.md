Thursday brings the last Epic Games Store swap of November, and it is a co-op charmer: **Bread & Fred**, the two-penguin climbing game, goes free on November 26 at 11 AM ET. The **Firestone** welcome bundle — a $100 pile of in-game items, not a game — is free to grab the same day. On Prime Gaming, **Kiborg** and **Trash Goblin** hit their last chance on December 2, while Amazon's December lineup of 14 more free games starts rolling out. Below are this week's freebies, their claim deadlines, and — if you'd rather play something *right now* — free Christmas browser games that need no download at all.

**One important note first:** the games below are full PC downloads you claim through the Epic Games Store or Amazon Luna — they are _not_ browser games, and you'll need a free Epic account to claim them. If you want instant play with zero installs, skip to the [Christmas browser games](#want-instant-play-free-christmas-browser-games) at the bottom.

**And a correction on last week:** an earlier version of our November 19 roundup listed **Alien: Isolation** as free on Epic. That listing came from early Epic feed data, but the game never appeared on Epic's free games page — only Mark of the Deep went free that week. We checked Epic's free games page on November 25 to confirm, and the older post has been corrected.

## Epic Games Store — Free November 26 — December 3

### Bread & Fred (free from Nov 26)

The week's headline, and the rare free game that is better with a friend. You play Bread and Fred, two penguins tied together by a rope, climbing a snowy mountain one timed jump at a time. The rope is the whole game: swing your partner across gaps, cling to walls together, and use each other's momentum — but one slip sends both of you tumbling back down the slope. A solo mode exists, but the game is clearly built for two players arguing, laughing, and blaming each other. Normally $14.99 — free to keep from November 26 at 11 AM ET through December 3 at 11 AM ET. _(As listed by Epic; free November 26 — December 3.)_

[Claim Bread & Fred free on Epic](https://store.epicgames.com/en-US/free-games)

### Firestone – Idle Clicker Online RPG welcome bundle (free Nov 26 — Dec 3)

Read this one carefully: Firestone itself is a free-to-play idle RPG, so Epic is not giving away a game here — it is giving away a **welcome gift bundle** advertised as worth $100. The bundle includes the Gladiator skin for Talia, 2 avatars, 10 common chests, and 5 uncommon chests. If you already play Firestone, or want to try it with a head start, claim the bundle from the game's Epic page while the window is open, November 26 at 11 AM ET through December 3 at 11 AM ET. _(As listed by Epic; bundle free November 26 — December 3.)_

[See this week's Epic freebies](https://store.epicgames.com/en-US/free-games)

### Last chance: Mark of the Deep (ends Nov 26, 11 AM ET)

The pirate Metroidvania from last week leaves Epic's free lineup Thursday morning. You play a pirate chasing the crew that betrayed you across a cursed archipelago, unlocking new abilities that open up earlier areas, and fighting bosses that punish button-mashing. Normally $19.99 — claim it before 11 AM ET on November 26 or it's gone. _(As listed by Epic; free November 19 — 26.)_

[Claim Mark of the Deep free on Epic](https://store.epicgames.com/en-US/p/mark-of-the-deep-f7dc08)

### On mobile: Lonesome Village

Epic's mobile giveaway banner currently shows **Lonesome Village** as the free game on the Epic app for Android — a cozy life-sim about rebuilding a village with a puzzle twist. No end date is shown on the page, so check the app itself for the deadline.

## Prime Gaming — Last chances and the December lineup

Amazon's November Prime games are winding down, and the December batch is starting to roll out. Prime members claim these on the Luna claims page; the codes redeem on the Epic Games Store, GOG, or the Amazon Games App, and the games are yours to keep.

### Last chance: Kiborg and Trash Goblin (claim by Dec 2)

Two very different games, both expiring Wednesday, December 2. **Kiborg** is a brutal roguelite beat 'em up — you play a prisoner serving a 1,300-year sentence, fighting through runs with melee combos, shooting, and character upgrades. **Trash Goblin** is the opposite mood entirely: a cozy shopkeeping game about cleaning, restoring, and selling unusual items. Both arrived October 29, and both go away December 2. _(As listed by Amazon; claim by December 2.)_

### Still claimable from November

Several November Prime games still have time on the clock: **WRC Generations** and **Squirrel with a Gun** until December 9; **Jurassic World Evolution 2**, **Radiolight**, and **Caravan SandWitch** until December 16; **Werewolf: The Apocalypse – Earthblood** and **Clem** until December 23; **Arcadia Fallen** until December 28; and **WarBreeds** until January 8. If you missed any of these in their launch weeks, this is your second chance.

### Just missed: Ad Infinitum

**Ad Infinitum**, the World War I-set first-person horror adventure, expired November 25, along with the puzzle game **Time Travel: Escape Room Game**. Both were claimable since October 22. If you grabbed them in time, they are yours to keep.

### Coming next: Amazon's December lineup (14 games)

Amazon has announced 14 free games for Prime members through December, rolling out in batches: **Lego 2K Drive**, **Forgotten Realms: The Archives – Collection One**, and **GYLT** first, then **Forgotten Realms: The Archives – Collection Two** and **Christmas Adventure: Candy Storm** on December 11, **Gun Slugs 2**, **Ashworld**, **Forgotten Realms: The Archives – Collection Three**, and **Bo: Path of the Teal Lotus** on December 18, **Fallout** and **Fallout 2** on December 23, and **Dreamscaper** plus **Living Legends: The Crystal Tears** on December 30. **Deus Ex: Mankind Divided** is also in the month's lineup. Check the Luna claims page for whatever is live right now — the batches unlock through the month, not all at once. _(As announced by Amazon; December lineup.)_
